MBA StrategyMBAD 560-01 · Strategic Management · Fall 2026

Why do some firms outperform others — and can that advantage last?

Step into the CEO's chair. This is a course about the choices that decide whether a company wins — where to compete, how to win, and what makes an advantage endure. You'll learn to think like a leader who owns the process and the outcome, putting rigorous frameworks to work on real decisions through five landmark cases, five decision-driven simulations, and a semester-long live client engagement.

2 credits · Full semester Mon / Wed · Aug 17 – Nov 23 Case-discussion led First MBA strategy course

Instructors  ·  Hadi Shaheen, PhD shaheenh@cofc.edu  ·  Kamyar Goudarzi, PhD goudarzik@cofc.edu

I
Diagnosis

External Environment & Competitive Advantage

Sessions 1–6
  • What strategy is (and isn't) — firm heterogeneity; sustainable advantage
  • Industry structure & value capture — Five Forces reframed — and their limits
  • Positioning & competitive advantage — willingness-to-pay↔cost wedge, differentiation vs. cost, isolating mechanisms
II
Value & Advantage

Business Models, Platforms & Capabilities

Sessions 7–16
  • Business models — how firms create, deliver & capture value (Business Model Canvas; freemium & business-model innovation)
  • Platforms & network effects — business-model innovation, disruption
  • Resources & dynamic capabilities — RBV/VRIO, isolating mechanisms, value chain
  • Durable advantage & ecosystems — value innovation (Blue Ocean), platform/ecosystem moats, scale, complementors
III
Scope & Execution

Scope, Global & Implementation

Sessions 17–22
  • Corporate scope & uncertainty — diversification, parenting advantage, real options
  • Global & beyond-market — CAGE/distance, market entry; non-market/stakeholder strategy
  • Firm boundaries & dynamics — make-vs-buy, M&A/alliances, rival response
  • Implementing strategy — leading change, power & influence; corporate governance

Application — real firms, real decisions

We explore some of today's most exciting and consequential industries — from premium dairy and global wine to Big Tech's AI race, AI chips, edtech, cloud computing, and digital platforms — and apply the best strategic thinking to real companies and the decisions their leaders actually face. Everything then comes together in a live engagement with a real client.

The Capstone — a top-tier consulting engagement for a real local business

The centerpiece of the course. Working in teams, you take on a genuine strategic challenge for a real Charleston-area company — the kind of engagement a top consulting firm would run. You meet your client, diagnose the real problem, gather your own evidence, analyze the problem, and build a recommendation you can stand behind. It culminates in a professional consulting-style report and a final presentation to a panel of the client and faculty — real stakes, real audience, real impact.

Form & scope→ ★ Client kickoff→ Collect & analyze→ ★ Mid-point→ Strategize→ ◆ Final panel
Five real local client companies across a range of industries — one live strategic challenge per company, sourced through the MBA program.
