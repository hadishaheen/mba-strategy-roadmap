MBA StrategyMBAD 560-01 · Strategic Management · Fall 2026

Why do some firms outperform others — and can that advantage last?

The whole course is one sustained answer to that question. The best strategic thinking comes to life through real cases, four hands-on simulations, and a live client engagement — never as a checklist of tools.

2 credits · Express I Mon / Wed · Aug 17 – Sep 30 Case-discussion led First MBA strategy course

Instructors  ·  Hadi Shaheen, PhD shaheenh@cofc.edu  ·  Kamyar Goudarzi, PhD goudarzik@cofc.edu

I
Diagnosis

External Environment & Value Capture

Sessions 1–3
  • What strategy is (and isn't) — firm heterogeneity; sustainable advantage
  • Industry structure & macro-environment — Five Forces, PESTEL — and their limits
  • Value creation vs. value capture — willingness-to-pay↔cost wedge, added value, positioning vs. cost
II
Advantage

Advantage & Firm Boundaries

Sessions 5–7
  • Resources, capabilities & dynamic capabilities — RBV/VRIO, isolating mechanisms, value chain
  • Firm boundaries — theory of the firm, make-vs-buy, vertical integration
  • Competitive dynamics — entry deterrence, rival response, co-opetition & complementors
  • Platforms & business models — network effects, business-model innovation, disruption
III
Scope & Context

Scope, Uncertainty & the Broader Context

Sessions 9–10
  • Corporate scope — diversification logic, parenting/corporate advantage, synergy
  • Strategy under uncertainty — real options, framing & behavioral strategy
  • Global & beyond-market — CAGE/distance, market entry; non-market/stakeholder; strategy as process
Woven throughout

Application — real firms, real decisions

We explore some of today's most exciting and consequential industries — from premium dairy and global wine to Big Tech's AI race, cloud computing, and digital platforms — and apply the best strategic thinking to real companies and the decisions their leaders actually face. Everything then comes together in a live engagement with a real client.
